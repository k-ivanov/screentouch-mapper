# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-30

First release.

- Menu bar app that pairs an absolute-pointer (mouse-mode) touchscreen with a display.
- Tap, drag and double-tap on the paired display.
- Flip Horizontally / Flip Vertically per device.
- Pairings survive replugs, restarts and display rearrangement.
- The device goes back to macOS when its display disconnects or the app quits.
- Start at Login.
- `--list` diagnostic for devices and displays.
- App icon.
